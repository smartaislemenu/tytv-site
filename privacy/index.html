TYTV Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

TYTV is a playlist player for Android TV (including Onn and Fire TV), iPhone and iPad, and a browser player at tytvapp.com/watch. It does not include any channels or content, and it does not require a TYTV account to download the Android app. We collect no personal data beyond what the app or site needs to work, and we do not sell, share or use any data for advertising or tracking.

What stays on your device

  • The playlists you add (M3U links, or an Xtream Codes server, username and password) are stored only on your device. On iPhone/iPad they use the iOS Keychain. On Android TV they stay in the app’s private storage. They are never sent to us.
  • Channel, movie and show lists, the TV guide, your favorites, recently watched items and resume positions are stored only on your device. Deleting a playlist or the app removes them.
  • TYTV connects directly to the playlist servers you add to load lists, the TV guide, artwork and video. Those servers are run by your provider, not by us, and their own privacy policies apply.

Web player (tytvapp.com/watch)

The browser player keeps playlist settings in your browser’s local storage on that device. Unlock uses an email and one-time code when that service is live; until then the unlock screen shows that it’s coming soon. We do not put third-party ad or analytics scripts on the TYTV website.

TV guide and sports scores

To show what is on now and the teams playing in a game, TYTV downloads the TV guide from your playlist provider and public sports scoreboards (for example ESPN and TheSportsDB). These scoreboard requests contain no personal information, playlist details or account data; like any website, those services can see the request's IP address.

If you tap the optional “Try a sample stream” button, TYTV loads public test streams from Apple’s servers (devstreaming-cdn.apple.com); like any website, Apple can see the request’s IP address, and no personal information is sent.

Crash reports (optional)

If “Send crash reports” is on (Settings), TYTV sends a crash or freeze report to our crash-reporting service (Sentry) so we can fix bugs. A report contains the app version, OS version, device model, a random install ID and technical details about the crash. Reports are scrubbed so they contain no web addresses, playlist details, usernames, passwords or IP addresses, and the service is told not to store the sender’s IP address. You can turn this off at any time in Settings.

This website

tytvapp.com is a static download and help site. It does not set tracking cookies. Basic server logs from our host (Cloudflare) may include IP addresses for security and reliability, which we do not use for advertising.
