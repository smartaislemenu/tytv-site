Install guides / Android phone or tablet

Android phone or tablet

Sideload the same APK on a phone or tablet

  1. Allow installs on your device

    On most phones: open the downloaded file and Android will ask you to allow that source. Or go to Settings → Apps → Special app access → Install unknown apps and allow your browser or Files app.

  2. Download TYTV

    In Chrome (or another browser) visit tytvapp.com and tap Download for Android TV. The same APK works on phones and tablets, though the UI is built for a TV remote.

  3. Install and open

    Open the APK from your notifications or Downloads folder and tap Install. Then open TYTV and add your playlist.

Heads-up: TYTV is designed for the living-room remote (10-foot UI). It works on phones, but for everyday couch use an Onn box, Google TV, Fire Stick, or Shield is a better fit.
